# Spawn vs. Cenobites: The Unclaimed

Complete 32-page one-shot script • Unofficial crossover fan comic • Draft 1

## Premise
At the height of his divine power, Al Simmons follows a grieving woman into Leviathan's Labyrinth. He can destroy its chains, but its captives have been conditioned to fear freedom. Pinhead's real wager: force a saviour to become a master.

## Continuity and rules
This is an original crossover, not a claim about shared official canon. Spawn uses his temporary Armageddon-era divine power throughout; no power theft or arbitrary depowering. Pinhead combines his familiar screen identity with the broader supernatural threat of the comics; we do not stack incompatible continuities or assert unlimited power. Leviathan participates as the Cenobites' ruler, not as a Cenobite. The mechanisms of the soul bargains, truth windows, and boundaries here are invented for this story. Physical victory belongs decisively to Spawn. The emotional test is helping prisoners choose freedom. Spawn could impose an outcome, but refuses to rewrite people's minds. The ending defeats this attempted claim and shields the exits; it does not claim Leviathan is permanently destroyed.

## Visual bible
Portrait American comic page, 2:3 aspect ratio. Premium painted supernatural horror comic, intricate black ink contours, dramatic chiaroscuro, textured print colors, crisp cinematic compositions. Consistent Spawn: muscular black living suit with bold white chest-and-face markings, green luminous eyes, large ragged crimson cape, silver skull clasp and heavy chains; divine state adds a thin white-gold halo behind shoulders, retains iconic suit. Consistent Pinhead: pale bald adult male Hell Priest with precisely gridded head pins, black leather ceremonial robes, controlled expression, black eyes; blue-white cold light. Mara Vale: adult woman, 32, short dark wavy hair, olive skin, dark teal coat, mustard scarf, small silver ring on right hand. Cenobites include Chatterer, Butterball, and the Female in recognizable original designs. Leviathan is an immense hovering black elongated octahedron above labyrinth. Palette: charcoal, bone, crimson, emerald green, cold blue, rare white gold. Horror and supernatural combat without explicit organs or sexual content. Rectangular panels, clean pale gutters, generously sized traditional white speech balloons and black uppercase lettering. Exact dialogue, no added text. Read left to right top to bottom. Keep all content within trim. Small footer page number. This is an unofficial crossover fan comic, no publisher logos.
Daniel Vale: adult man of 35, dark curls, olive skin, plain grey shirt. Mara is his younger adult sister. Wanda appears once as an illusory adult woman in a cream dress, visibly distinct from Mara. The shore is a threshold for the dead, not evidence that all souls are resurrected. Mara alone returns to living New York.

## Page 1 — The invitation

**Panel 1.** Wide rain-soaked New York alley at midnight. Mara kneels beside an old man's empty coat. A brass puzzle cube rests on the fabric.

CAPTION: NEW YORK. 2:13 A.M.

**Panel 2.** Close on Mara's hand and the cube. In its polished brass she sees the reflection of her adult brother reaching from darkness.

VOICE FROM BOX: MARA. PLEASE.

**Panel 3.** Mara turns one segment. In the puddle beneath her, a vast red cape is reflected although no one stands above it.

SPAWN (OFF): TAKE YOUR HAND OFF THAT.

## Page 2 — A door opens

**Panel 1.** Mara recoils. Spawn steps from the brick shadow, green eyes bright in rain.

MARA: MY BROTHER'S IN THERE.

**Panel 2.** The cube floats between them. A needle-thin blue doorway splits the alley.

SPAWN: SOMETHING WANTS YOU TO THINK HE IS.

**Panel 3.** Full-width reveal: Pinhead, Chatterer, Butterball and the Female stand inside the impossible doorway. Pinhead studies Spawn.

PINHEAD: AND SOMETHING WANTS YOU TO PLAY SAVIOUR.

## Page 3 — Terms

**Panel 1.** Mara stands behind Spawn's cape. Pinhead's chains stop inches from Spawn's face.

PINHEAD: SHE ASKED. WE ANSWERED.

**Panel 2.** Mara holds up a scorched hospital bracelet.

MARA: HE DIED IN A FIRE. THE BOX SAID I COULD BRING HIM BACK.

**Panel 3.** Spawn grips a hovering hook. It melts into emerald sparks in his palm.

SPAWN: THEN IT LIED.

## Page 4 — The first refusal

**Panel 1.** Chatterer lunges with hooked chains. Spawn catches them in his cape, plants his feet.

SFX: KRRANG

**Panel 2.** A controlled green pulse sends the three attendant Cenobites back through the doorway. Pinhead remains still.

SPAWN: GO HOME.

**Panel 3.** Close on Pinhead. His pins reflect a white-gold halo forming behind Spawn.

PINHEAD: YOU CAN BREAK EVERY CHAIN HERE. CAN YOU TELL WHICH ONES SHE WANTS?

## Page 5 — A name in the dark

**Panel 1.** Pinhead opens a window of blue light: Mara's adult brother Daniel sits upright, wrapped in a grey shroud in a stone cell.

DANIEL: MARA?

**Panel 2.** Mara steps toward the window. Spawn gently stops her with an open hand.

MARA: THAT'S HIS VOICE.

**Panel 3.** Spawn looks past the window into endless corridors. Across their walls, tiny human silhouettes.

SPAWN: HOW MANY? PINHEAD: YOU HAVE TIME TO COUNT.

## Page 6 — The bargain refused

**Panel 1.** Pinhead extends one pale hand toward Spawn's luminous chest.

PINHEAD: LEAVE YOUR LIGHT AT THE THRESHOLD. TAKE HER BROTHER.

**Panel 2.** Spawn's halo broadens. Rain freezes motionless around him; the Cenobite doorway bends under pressure.

SPAWN: YOU DON'T GET TO SET MY LIMITS.

**Panel 3.** He steps through at full power, holding the doorway open behind Mara. Pinhead follows, unruffled.

PINHEAD: NO. ONLY YOUR CHOICES.

## Page 7 — The Labyrinth

**Panel 1.** Nearly full-page vista: Spawn and Mara on a narrow bridge over an immense labyrinth of black stone. Leviathan hangs above. Tiny human lights in walls. Pinhead stands far ahead.

CAPTION: SOME PRISONS HAVE NO OUTSIDE.

**Panel 2.** Inset close on Mara at Spawn's side, staring down toward the lights.

MARA: THOSE ARE PEOPLE.

**Panel 3.** Inset Spawn's green eyes reflect the endless maze.

SPAWN: I KNOW.

## Page 8 — A god at the gate

**Panel 1.** Spawn rises above the bridge, halo incandescent. He opens his hand toward the prison walls.

SPAWN: LET THEM GO.

**Panel 2.** Huge emerald fissures open through the walls. Human spirits float free, astonished; no injuries.

SFX: KRAAAM

**Panel 3.** Several spirits recoil in terror and rush back into the dark cells. Mara reaches helplessly toward them.

SPIRIT: DON'T MAKE US REMEMBER.

## Page 9 — What power cannot answer

**Panel 1.** Spawn catches falling masonry without effort. A frightened adult spirit curls behind its broken cell door.

MARA: YOU OPENED IT. WHY WON'T THEY LEAVE?

**Panel 2.** Pinhead walks between floating fragments of stone.

PINHEAD: PAIN IS FAMILIAR. HOPE REQUIRES RISK.

**Panel 3.** Spawn looks toward him, fury held in check.

SPAWN: YOU TAUGHT THEM THAT.

## Page 10 — The map

**Panel 1.** Spawn lowers to the floor. Thin green threads connect his fingertips to scores of sealed cells.

SPAWN: DANIEL VALE.

**Panel 2.** All threads bend toward a staircase far below. One thread briefly forms Daniel's frightened face.

MARA: CAN YOU REACH HIM?

**Panel 3.** Spawn closes his fist; a direct passage opens through the maze.

SPAWN: YES. STAY CLOSE.

## Page 11 — The procession

**Panel 1.** Spawn and Mara walk through the new passage. Rows of motionless Cenobites fill both sides like cathedral statues.

CAPTION: THE LABYRINTH DOES NOT LIKE STRAIGHT LINES.

**Panel 2.** Butterball blocks the path. Chatterer crawls across the ceiling. The Female extends a web of hooks.

FEMALE: NOTHING LEAVES UNCHANGED.

**Panel 3.** Spawn's cape sweeps protectively around Mara; his fists flare.

SPAWN: MOVE.

## Page 12 — Chains against chains

**Panel 1.** Action wide: Chatterer's black chains crash against Spawn's silver chains. Mara shelters inside the red cape.

SFX: KLANG

**Panel 2.** Spawn folds a stone wall around Butterball like a closing book, restraining him without graphic harm.

SFX: GRRROM

**Panel 3.** The Female hooks the hem of Spawn's cape. Spawn turns, letting the fabric dissolve into green smoke around her hooks.

SPAWN: YOU'RE PULLING THE WRONG THREAD.

## Page 13 — The Hell Priest

**Panel 1.** Pinhead steps into the smoke. Hundreds of cold blue chains radiate outward, suspending chunks of the corridor.

PINHEAD: THEY WERE NEVER MEANT TO STOP YOU.

**Panel 2.** Spawn recognizes Mara is no longer beside him. A small mustard thread from her scarf drifts in his palm.

SPAWN: MARA.

**Panel 3.** Mara stands in an apparently warm hospital room. Daniel lies in the bed, alive and smiling.

DANIEL: YOU CAME BACK.

## Page 14 — The room

**Panel 1.** Mara embraces Daniel beside the hospital bed. Through the window, the sky is pale and calm.

MARA: I THOUGHT I'D LOST YOU.

**Panel 2.** Close: Daniel's wrist bears a hospital bracelet, its printed letters are illegible marks. Mara's smile fades.

MARA: WHAT DID YOU CALL ME WHEN WE WERE KIDS?

**Panel 3.** Daniel's smile becomes fixed. Fine black cracks appear in the false daylight.

DANIEL: DOES IT MATTER IF YOU'RE HAPPY?

## Page 15 — No

**Panel 1.** Mara steps back from the imitation. The room peels into shadow.

MARA: HE WOULD HAVE ANSWERED.

**Panel 2.** She removes her mustard scarf from the imitation's grasp. An honest, terrified silhouette is visible behind the false wall.

MARA: I WANT MY BROTHER. NOT THIS.

**Panel 3.** Spawn tears the false room open from outside with both hands. His cape fills the breach.

SPAWN: THEN LET'S FIND HIM.

## Page 16 — The real prisoner

**Panel 1.** Spawn and Mara enter a spare stone cell. The real Daniel sits on a bench, adult man with dark curls and a plain grey shirt.

DANIEL: DON'T COME CLOSER.

**Panel 2.** Mara stops immediately, hands open. Spawn stays back by the door.

MARA: ALL RIGHT. I'M HERE.

**Panel 3.** Close on Daniel's hands. A translucent brass chain passes from his wrist into the wall.

DANIEL: THEY SAID YOU'D BURN IF I LEFT.

## Page 17 — The signature

**Panel 1.** Pinhead appears in the cell doorway behind Spawn.

PINHEAD: HE ASKED TO SAVE HER. WE PROVIDED THE TERMS.

**Panel 2.** Flashback in ember tones: adult Daniel trapped in smoke reaches for a cube; a distant image shows Mara unconscious outside. Avoid wounds.

DANIEL (CAPTION): I SAID YES. I DIDN'T KNOW WHAT TO.

**Panel 3.** Spawn examines the translucent chain. Inside each link are small reflected images of Mara.

SPAWN: FEAR ISN'T A CONTRACT.

## Page 18 — The temptation

**Panel 1.** Pinhead gestures. A luminous apparition of Wanda appears beside Spawn, gently reaching out. She wears a simple cream dress.

PINHEAD: YOU COULD RESTORE HER TOO.

**Panel 2.** Close on Spawn. One emerald tear of light traces his mask. His hands remain lowered.

WANDA'S IMAGE: AL.

**Panel 3.** He turns away from the apparition toward Mara and Daniel.

SPAWN: SHE ISN'T YOURS TO OFFER.

## Page 19 — The verdict of Leviathan

**Panel 1.** Leviathan rotates overhead. The roof of the cell unfolds into a vast black cathedral open to the maze.

SFX: VOOOOM

**Panel 2.** Cenobite ranks appear along the galleries. Pinhead stands before a giant door made of interlocking chains.

PINHEAD: OUR GOD WOULD HEAR YOUR OBJECTION.

**Panel 3.** Spawn rises into the air, no longer concealing his white-gold radiance. Mara and Daniel stand protected below.

SPAWN: THEN IT CAN LISTEN.

## Page 20 — Peak

**Panel 1.** Large upper panel: Spawn's cape spans the cathedral like crimson wings. Green lightning and white-gold light circle his black and white form.

SPAWN: I HAVE SEEN WHAT GODS DO WHEN THEY CALL PEOPLE PROPERTY.

**Panel 2.** Lower left: thousands of hooked chains launch from the galleries toward him.

SFX: SHRAAAK

**Panel 3.** Lower right: every hook stops in midair inches from Spawn. He looks directly toward Leviathan.

SPAWN: ONCE WAS ENOUGH.

## Page 21 — The breaking

**Panel 1.** Spawn closes his hand. The frozen chain storm collapses into harmless silver dust.

SFX: KSHHH

**Panel 2.** Pinhead attacks with a concentrated spear of black-blue energy. Spawn intercepts it with one open palm.

PINHEAD: YOU MISTAKE DEFIANCE FOR FREEDOM.

**Panel 3.** Spawn drives the attack into the empty floor. The cathedral splits around the protected humans.

SPAWN: AND YOU MISTAKE SUFFERING FOR TRUTH.

## Page 22 — A door for everyone

**Panel 1.** Spawn's light draws an immense doorway over the maze. Beyond is a quiet shore beneath a dawn sky, not a resurrected city.

SPAWN: THERE'S YOUR EXIT.

**Panel 2.** Thousands of spirits approach, then recoil as Leviathan casts a black beam over them. The beam shows fearful personal memories.

SPIRIT: IT WILL FOLLOW US.

**Panel 3.** Mara takes Daniel's hand without pulling him. Spawn sees them from above.

MARA: THEN DON'T GO ALONE.

## Page 23 — The offer beneath the offer

**Panel 1.** Pinhead, robes wind-torn but composed, speaks to Spawn amid levitating stone.

PINHEAD: YOU COULD MAKE THEM WALK. ONE THOUGHT.

**Panel 2.** Spawn sees a vision of obedient, vacant-eyed souls marching through his doorway like puppets.

PINHEAD (OFF): YOU COULD MAKE THEM GRATEFUL.

**Panel 3.** He shatters the vision with a sweep of his hand, visibly disgusted.

SPAWN: THAT'S WHAT YOU WANT.

## Page 24 — The trap named

**Panel 1.** Close on Pinhead, shadows between the pins.

PINHEAD: EVERY SAVIOUR BECOMES A MASTER. GIVEN ENOUGH TIME.

**Panel 2.** Spawn descends beside the prisoners instead of looming above them. His halo remains bright.

SPAWN: THEN WATCH.

**Panel 3.** He opens a small door beside Daniel. It shows the real fire, Mara already outside, alive with rescuers.

SPAWN: HERE'S WHAT THEY HID.

## Page 25 — The truth

**Panel 1.** Daniel watches the true memory: firefighters carry Mara away before he touches the cube. No burns shown.

DANIEL: YOU WERE ALREADY OUT.

**Panel 2.** Mara holds his hands, tears on her face.

MARA: YOU DIDN'T OWE THEM ANYTHING.

**Panel 3.** Daniel looks from the memory to his translucent chain. He stands.

DANIEL: I WANT TO LEAVE.

## Page 26 — The unclaimed

**Panel 1.** The chain snaps away from Daniel without injuring him. Spawn holds its broken link in his palm.

SFX: TINK

**Panel 2.** Spawn sends the true memory outward as windows of light. Other souls see the hidden circumstances of their own bargains.

SPAWN: LOOK. THEN CHOOSE.

**Panel 3.** An adult woman spirit leaves a cell voluntarily. Then another prisoner follows. Some remain inside.

CAPTION: THE FIRST STEP BELONGED TO THEM.

## Page 27 — The final assault

**Panel 1.** Leviathan's black beam converges on the escaping spirits. Spawn places himself between beam and doorway.

SFX: VROOOOM

**Panel 2.** Huge central clash: his red cape forms a shelter over the spirits; his green-white light drives the beam back toward the octahedron.

SPAWN: YOU DON'T GET A SECOND CLAIM.

**Panel 3.** Pinhead hurls his final hooked chain toward Daniel. Mara sees it; Spawn's own silver chain catches it mid-flight.

SFX: KLANG

## Page 28 — Judgment

**Panel 1.** Spawn lands directly before Pinhead. The Hell Priest's chains lie severed around him.

PINHEAD: WILL YOU DESTROY OUR WORLD?

**Panel 2.** Spawn holds one hand open. Each visible cell has an independent doorway, glowing softly.

SPAWN: I'M ENDING YOUR HOLD ON THESE PEOPLE.

**Panel 3.** Wide: Leviathan recedes behind a white-gold boundary as Spawn seals its reach away from the exits. No claim of killing it.

SPAWN: THE REST IS YOURS TO LIVE WITH.

## Page 29 — The shore

**Panel 1.** On the quiet threshold shore, Daniel looks through one door toward living New York and another toward a warm, distant horizon.

DANIEL: CAN I COME HOME?

**Panel 2.** Spawn kneels to meet his gaze, hands open, no false promise.

SPAWN: I CAN MAKE A BODY. I WON'T CHOOSE YOUR LIFE FOR YOU.

**Panel 3.** Daniel looks at Mara, relieved rather than terrified.

DANIEL: I THINK I'VE BEEN HOLDING ON BECAUSE I THOUGHT YOU NEEDED ME TO.

## Page 30 — Goodbye

**Panel 1.** Mara and Daniel embrace at the shore, Spawn in silhouette at a respectful distance.

MARA: I NEED YOU. BUT YOU DON'T HAVE TO HURT FOR ME.

**Panel 2.** Daniel steps toward the warm horizon with other freed spirits. He looks back and smiles.

DANIEL: NIGHT, MAGPIE.

**Panel 3.** Mara smiles through tears as she recognizes his childhood nickname for her.

MARA: NIGHT, DAN.

## Page 31 — The box

**Panel 1.** Return to the wet alley. Dawn. Mara and Spawn stand beside the empty coat. The brass puzzle cube lies in Spawn's hand.

MARA: IS IT OVER?

**Panel 2.** Spawn opens his palm. The cube becomes an ordinary smooth brass block with no seams.

SPAWN: THIS DOOR IS.

**Panel 3.** Mara walks toward daylight, her mustard scarf catching the breeze. Spawn watches from shadow.

MARA: THANK YOU FOR ASKING HIM.

## Page 32 — The answer

**Panel 1.** In the Labyrinth, Pinhead stands before Leviathan amid rows of empty cells. A few souls sit behind open doors.

PINHEAD: HE COULD HAVE TAKEN THE THRONE.

**Panel 2.** Close on a discarded broken hook lying beside an open cell. Above it, Pinhead's pale hand remains empty.

PINHEAD (OFF): HE LEFT THE DOORS.

**Panel 3.** Final wide panel: Spawn on a New York rooftop, crimson cape against sunrise. Tiny green eyes; white-gold halo faint but present.

CAPTION: POWER CAN OPEN A DOOR. CAPTION: FREEDOM IS LETTING SOMEONE CHOOSE. FOOTER: THE END.
