About the comic

Spawn vs. Cenobites: The Unclaimed is an original, unofficial crossover fan comic created for Nutty: one cover, 32 story pages and 96 panels. Story, panel script and artwork were created with ChatGPT; illustrations are AI-generated.

Spawn was created by Todd McFarlane. Hellraiser and its Cenobites were created by Clive Barker. Their respective owners retain their characters. This story invents its crossover rules and is not official continuity.

Contains supernatural horror, disturbing imagery, bereavement and fantasy violence.
